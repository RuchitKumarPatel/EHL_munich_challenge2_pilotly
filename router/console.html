What does lower friction cost?

Each point is a complete policy over all 1,000 trajectories, measured again. X is the estimated, cache-aware cost. Y is a lower bound on the Composite Outcome Proxy: 1 − badness − 95% upper bound on the increase. Deliberately no point estimate—the MDE is 11.4 pp, so a point would be noise dressed as precision. The band above is the Manski widening: the cost of trajectories whose target arm was never observed in their stratum.
